**Last updated:** 2026-11-16

## English (Google Play Store Review)

### 1. Overview
Click Planet is a free arcade/clicker game with **no real-money purchases**, **no advertisements**, and **no third-party analytics SDKs**.

### 2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device
Your game progress (scores, upgrades, preferences, and optional custom server URL) is stored locally on your device using browser/WebView `localStorage`. This local data never leaves your device unless you choose to sync a highscore.

### 3. Online Highscore Leaderboard (Optional)
If online highscores are enabled (via the developer's Cloudflare Worker API), the app sends only:
- A randomly generated anonymous `deviceId` (created locally on first launch to distinguish players)
- Your chosen display name / nickname (default: `Player`)
- Your numeric game score

This information is stored solely in the developer's Cloudflare D1 leaderboard database to display public rankings inside the app. We do **not** collect your real name, email address, location, contacts, device IMEI, or advertising ID.

### 4. Data Sharing & Selling
We do not sell, rent, or share any user data with advertisers, data brokers, or third parties.

### 5. Data Deletion
- **Local data:** Clear app storage or uninstall the app to remove all local progress.
- **Leaderboard entry:** You can contact the developer via the GitHub repository issues page (`https://github.com/VaelNTN/click-planet/issues`) with your display name to request removal of a leaderboard entry.

## Nederlands

### 1. Overzicht
Click Planet is een gratis game zonder advertenties, zonder trackers van derden en zonder aankopen met echt geld.

### 2. Lokale opslag
Je spelvoortgang en gekozen naam worden lokaal opgeslagen op je toestel (`localStorage`).

### 3. Online Highscores
Wanneer de online highscore-server actief is, verstuurt de app alleen:
- Een willekeurig lokaal aangemaakt `deviceId`
- Je gekozen spelersnaam (bijv. `Player`)
- Je behaalde score

Er worden geen e-mailadressen, persoonsgegevens, locatiegegevens of advertentie-ID's verzameld of gedeeld met derden.
